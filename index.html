UNIT DEFENCE / PLAY LABFour original 2D gameplay prototypes · mouse + keyboard

Move the squad. Change the outcome.

Four short experiments in tactical survival. Every unit is yours to command. Pick a scenario, learn your specialists, and reshape the squad between encounters.

ZOMBIE · FIXED DEFENCE

01 / Last Light

A supply depot, an exposed perimeter, and six increasingly dangerous attacks.

  • Build a firing line around cover and high ground.
  • Keep runners off your sniper and medic.
  • Deal with poison, callers, and a siege beast.
ZOMBIE · MOBILE DEFENCE

02 / Dead Road

Your repair truck is crossing hostile ground. A good position is only temporary.

  • Escort the truck through six checkpoints.
  • Move specialists as the battle moves.
  • Clear nearby enemies so the truck can advance.
ALIEN · EXPEDITION SQUAD

03 / Rift Wardens

Contain an alien incursion. Each encounter pulls your squad towards a different breach.

  • Occupy a rift for eight uncontested seconds.
  • Counter armoured shells and specialist hunters.
  • Upgrade support units to neutralise alien toxins.
ALIEN · PLAY AS THE BROOD

04 / The Living Front

A living colony faces harvesting machines. Grow your army from the remains of attackers.

  • Command fast skirmishers and tough shellguards.
  • Spend eight biomass to grow another skirmisher.
  • Regenerate around the heart and evolve the brood.
Controls: click or drag to select · Shift adds to selection · right-click to move / focus an enemy · A then click to advance and fight · Space tactical pause · H hold · Ctrl + A select all.
Units fight automatically in range. Snipers must stop to fire. You can give orders while paused. Six encounters per run; best progress stays on this browser.
